## Paragraph to paste into the dissertation (scope statement)

> The submitted study is narrower than the original project plan, and the narrowing is deliberate. Proposal-era notes described a seven-class backchannel taxonomy (nod, shake, tilt, lean, eyebrow raise, neutral) and a multimodal architecture combining VideoMAE with FLAME/EMOCA pose. What was executed and evaluated is a binary, pose-only study on 30 human-labelled RealTalk windows: a frozen EMOCA-pose amplitude rule (TEST precision 0.64, recall 0.70, F1 0.67) and a 1D CNN trained on 80 pseudo-labels produced by that rule (TEST precision 0.70, recall 0.70, F1 0.70), with the TEST set scored exactly once. EMOCA was streamed from official pickles, not trained. A follow-up frozen-VideoMAE-head check (frozen `videomae-base` embeddings plus a small MLP head on the same pseudo-labels) scored TEST F1 0.57 on the same held-out windows — below both pose systems — so the pose-only results stand as the contribution. VideoMAE fine-tuning, pose+VideoMAE fusion, and the seven-class taxonomy were not run: the lab account used for pose extraction has a 25 GB quota and about 6.5 GB remained after a CPU PyTorch install, which cannot hold RealTalk video shards plus a trainable VideoMAE backbone. These are scope and resource decisions, not failed experiments: no fusion, seven-class, or multiclass macro-F1 score is reported, and none is claimed.
