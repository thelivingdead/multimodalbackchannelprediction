## Themes and seed papers

### Non-verbal behaviour / backchannels / head gestures
- Lin et al., MM-F2F, ACL 2025. arXiv:2505.12654. Tri-modal Keep/Turn/Backchannel. Gap: fine-grained nod typing and weak supervision on RealTalk.

### RealTalk dataset
- Geng, Scott; Teotia, Revant; Tendulkar, Purva; Menon, Sachit; Vondrick, Carl. *Affective Faces for Goal-Driven Dyadic Communication*. 2023. Hugging Face dataset `scottgeng00/realtalk`. 692 dyadic videos, 25 fps, EMOCA tracks p0/p1.

### FLAME
- Li, Tianye; Bolkart, Timo; Black, Michael J.; Li, Hao; Romero, Javier. *Learning a model of facial shape and expression from 4D scans*. SIGGRAPH Asia 2017. FLAME 3D morphable head model.

### EMOCA / DECA
- Danecek, Radek; Black, Michael J.; Bolkart, Timo. *EMOCA: Emotion-Driven Monocular Capture of Facial Animation*. CVPR 2022.
- Feng, Yao; Feng, Haiwen; Black, Michael J.; Bolkart, Timo. *Learning an Animatable Detailed 3D Face Model from In-The-Wild Images* (DECA). SIGGRAPH 2021.

### VideoMAE
- Tong, Zhan; Song, Yibing; Wang, Jue; Wang, Limin. *VideoMAE: Masked Autoencoders are Data-Efficient Learners for Self-Supervised Video Pre-Training*. ECCV 2022.

### Weak supervision / pseudo-labelling
- Ratner, Alexander et al. *Snorkel: Rapid Training Data Creation with Weak Supervision*. VLDB 2017.
